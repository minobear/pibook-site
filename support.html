關於照片與隱私

Photos and privacy

我的照片會被上傳嗎？

不會。相似照分群、臉部偵測、品質評分、壓縮，全部在你的手機上完成，我們的伺服器上沒有你的任何照片。

唯一的例外是你主動訂購實體印製商品時，為那筆訂單選的照片才會上傳，用來製作商品，出貨後 90 天內刪除。

你們會辨識照片裡的人是誰嗎？

不會。臉部偵測只用來判斷「這張裡的臉清不清楚、眼睛有沒有閉起來」，好在一組相似照中挑出比較好的那張。我們不建立臉部特徵樣板，也不比對不同照片裡的人是不是同一個人，這些資料完全不離開你的裝置。

為什麼需要「完整」的相簿權限？

拍簿的核心是「幫你把整個相簿看過一遍」——找出相似的、重複的、模糊的。只給部分照片權限的話，App 只看得到你手動選的那幾張，整理與統計都會失去意義。

你當然可以只給部分權限，App 仍然可以運作，只是能幫上的忙有限。

我可以關掉使用數據回報嗎？

可以，隨時都行：「我的 → 偏好設定」裡有開關。關掉之後 App 不會再送出任何分析事件。

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Grouping, face detection, quality scoring and compression all run on your phone. Our servers hold none of your photos.

The one exception is when you order a printed product — the photos you chose for that order are uploaded to make it, and deleted within 90 days of dispatch.

Do you identify who is in my photos?

No. Face detection only judges whether faces are sharp and eyes are open, so we can pick the better shot from a group of similar ones. We build no face templates, never match people across photos, and none of it leaves your device.

Why does it need full photo library access?

Pibook’s job is to look through your whole library — finding the similar, the duplicated, the blurry. With limited access it only sees the handful you picked by hand, which makes the organising and the statistics meaningless.

You can still grant limited access and the app will work; it just cannot help as much.

Can I turn off usage analytics?

Yes, at any time — the switch is in Profile → Preferences. After that the app sends no analytics events at all.

訂閱與購買

Subscriptions and purchases

我換手機了，Premium 怎麼拿回來？

用同一個 Apple ID 或 Google 帳號登入商店，然後在 App 的 Premium 頁面按「還原購買」。

如果你當初有登入拍簿帳號，登入同一個帳號也會自動帶回權益。

怎麼取消訂閱？

iPhone／iPad：設定 → 你的姓名 → 訂閱項目 → 拍簿 → 取消訂閱。

Android：Google Play → 個人資料 → 付款與訂閱 → 訂閱項目 → 拍簿 → 取消。

取消後，Premium 會持續到你已付費的期間結束為止。在 App 內刪除帳號不會取消訂閱，一定要到上面的位置操作。

我想退款

App 內的購買款項由 Apple 或 Google 收取，我們收不到也退不了，必須向他們申請：

Apple 請至 reportaproblem.apple.com；Android 請於 Google Play 的訂單記錄中申請。

如果問題是 App 的缺陷造成的，先寫信告訴我們，我們會協助你向商店說明，也會在能力範圍內補償你。

付了錢但 Premium 沒開通

請先確認網路是通的，然後在 Premium 頁面按一次「還原購買」。多數情況這樣就好了。

仍然沒有的話，來信告訴我們你的購買時間與購買平台，我們會查明並手動為你開通。

I changed phones — how do I get Premium back?

Sign in to the store with the same Apple ID or Google account, then tap Restore Purchases on the Premium screen.

If you had signed in to a Pibook account, signing in again also brings your entitlement back automatically.

How do I cancel?

iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pibook → Cancel.

Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pibook → Cancel.

Premium continues until the period you already paid for ends. Deleting your account in the app does not cancel the subscription — it must be done in the places above.

I want a refund

In-app payments are collected by Apple or Google; we never receive them and cannot refund them. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: request it from your Google Play order history.

If a defect in the app caused the problem, write to us first — we will help explain it to the store and compensate you where we can.

I paid but Premium did not activate

Check your connection, then tap Restore Purchases on the Premium screen. That resolves most cases.

If it still does not appear, email us your purchase date and platform and we will look it up and activate it manually.

整理與刪除

Organising and deleting

我不小心刪掉照片了，救得回來嗎？

拍簿本身沒有你的照片副本，所以我們沒辦法直接幫你救回。但作業系統通常還留著：

iPhone：照片 App → 相簿 → 最近刪除（保留 30 天）。
Android：Google 相簿 → 收藏庫 → 垃圾桶（保留 30 或 60 天）。

建議整理前先確認你有可靠的雲端備份。

整理進度會不會不見？

進度存在你的手機上。如果你有登入並開啟雲端備份，換手機時可以還原 —— 我們用照片的「指紋」（由拍攝時間與檔案特徵推導的單向雜湊）認回同一張照片，因為系統相簿的照片編號換一台手機就完全不同了。

為什麼有些照片分析得比較慢？

如果照片只存在 iCloud 而不在本機，App 需要先向系統要一份下來，這一步的速度取決於你的網路。手機發燙時 App 也會主動放慢，避免影響你正在做的事。

怎麼刪除我的帳號？

「我的 → 帳號 → 刪除帳號」，或從這一頁提出申請。完整說明（會刪什麼、留什麼、多久）都在那裡。

I deleted a photo by mistake — can it be recovered?

Pibook holds no copy of your photos, so we cannot recover it directly. Your operating system usually still has it:

iPhone: Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted (kept 30 days).
Android: Google Photos → Library → Trash (kept 30 or 60 days).

We recommend confirming you have a reliable cloud backup before an organising session.

Will I lose my organising progress?

Progress lives on your phone. If you sign in and enable cloud backup you can restore it on a new device — we recognise the same photo by its fingerprint (a one-way hash of capture time and file characteristics), because system photo identifiers are completely different on a new phone.

Why are some photos slower to analyse?

If a photo lives in iCloud rather than on the device, the app has to ask the system to fetch it first, and that depends on your connection. The app also slows itself down when the phone gets warm, so it never gets in the way of what you are doing.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Account → Delete Account, or submit a request from this page, which explains exactly what is deleted, what is retained and how long it takes.
