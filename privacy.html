三十秒摘要

THE SHORT VERSION

  • 你的照片不會離開這台裝置。相似比對、臉部偵測、品質評分全部在手機本機執行。唯一的例外是你主動下訂實體印製商品時，被你選中的那幾張照片才會上傳，用來完成訂單。
  • 我們不做臉部辨識。臉部偵測只用來判斷「這張裡的人臉清不清楚」，結果留在裝置上，我們不建立、不儲存、不傳送任何臉部特徵資料。
  • 不登入也能用。登入是為了雲端備份與跨裝置的訂閱權益，不是使用條件。
  • 我們不販售你的資料，也不做跨 App 廣告追蹤。使用分析可以在 App 內一鍵關閉。
  • 帳號可以刪，而且是真的刪。App 內與這個網頁都能提出。
  • Your photos never leave your device. Grouping, face detection and quality scoring all run locally. The one exception: when you place an order for a printed product, the photos you selected are uploaded to fulfil that order.
  • We do not do facial recognition. Face detection is used only to judge whether faces in a shot are sharp. Results stay on your device. We never create, store or transmit facial templates.
  • You can use the app without an account. Signing in exists for cloud backup and cross-device subscription access — it is not a condition of use.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not run cross-app advertising tracking. Usage analytics can be switched off inside the app.
  • You can delete your account, and it really is deleted. Available in the app and on this page.

2照片為什麼不離開裝置Why your photos stay on the device

這是本 App 最核心的設計決定，不是一句行銷詞。本 App 的所有影像處理 —— 相似照分群、臉部偵測、清晰度與構圖評分、截圖與分類判斷、縮圖產生、 壓縮與轉檔 —— 全部在你的裝置上完成。這些工作不需要伺服器， 我們也沒有為它們建置任何伺服器。

因此：我們的伺服器上沒有你的任何照片或影片，也沒有從照片中萃取出的影像特徵。

關於臉部偵測

本 App 使用裝置端的機器學習模型偵測照片中是否有人臉、人臉是否清晰、眼睛是否張開， 用途只有一個：在一組相似照片中挑出比較好的那一張。 我們不做身分辨識、不建立臉部特徵樣板（face template）、 不比對不同照片中的人是不是同一個人， 也不會把任何臉部相關資料傳出裝置。偵測結果只存在你手機本機的資料庫中， 解除安裝 App 即隨之消失。

兩個必須說明的例外

例外一：你下訂實體印製商品時。 如果你在 App 內購買相片書、明信片等需要印製的實體商品， 你為該筆訂單所選擇的照片會被上傳到我們的伺服器，交付印製廠商完成製作。 這是履行你所下訂單的必要行為，除此之外我們不會將它們用於任何其他目的。 訂單完成後的保存期間請見第 7 節。

例外二：把座標變成地名時。 如果你的照片含有 GPS 座標，且你使用了依地點瀏覽的功能， 本 App 會呼叫你裝置作業系統內建的地理編碼服務，把座標換成可讀的地名。 這個查詢由 Apple 或 Google 處理，適用它們各自的隱私權政策。 我們不會儲存這些座標，也不會將它們傳送到我們的伺服器。

This is the App’s central design decision, not a marketing line. All image processing — similar-photo grouping, face detection, sharpness and composition scoring, screenshot and category classification, thumbnail generation, compression and transcoding — happens entirely on your device. None of it requires a server, and we have not built one for it.

As a result: our servers hold none of your photos or videos, and none of the image features extracted from them.

About face detection

The App uses an on-device machine-learning model to detect whether a photo contains faces, whether those faces are in focus, and whether eyes are open. It serves exactly one purpose: picking the better shot out of a group of similar ones. We do not perform identity recognition, we do not build face templates, and we do not match people across photos. No face-related data ever leaves your device. Detection results live only in a local database on your phone and disappear when you uninstall the App.

Two exceptions we must state clearly

Exception 1 — when you order a printed product. If you order a photo book, postcard or other printed item inside the App, the photos you selected for that order are uploaded to our servers and passed to the print partner who manufactures it. This is necessary to fulfil the order you placed; we use them for nothing else. Retention is covered in section 7.

Exception 2 — turning coordinates into place names. If your photos carry GPS coordinates and you use the place-based browsing feature, the App asks your operating system’s built-in geocoding service to convert those coordinates into a readable place name. That lookup is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We do not store the coordinates and do not send them to our servers.

為保護你的資料，我們可能需要先確認提出請求的人確實是你 —— 通常是請你以帳號註冊時的同一個電子郵件地址來信。

Wherever you are, you may ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your data (except where law requires us to keep it — see above);
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including switching analytics off at any time;
  • port your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

The fastest route is in the App: the analytics switch is in Profile → Preferences, and account deletion is in Profile → Account. Otherwise write to privacy@pibook.app and we will respond within 30 days, extendable by a further 30 days for complex requests with prior notice to you. We will not discriminate against you or degrade your service for exercising these rights.

To protect your data we may first need to verify that the request really comes from you — usually by asking you to write from the same email address your account uses.

11安全措施Security

  • 所有網路傳輸一律使用 TLS 加密。
  • 資料庫層採用列級安全性（Row Level Security）—— 每位使用者在資料庫層級就只讀得到自己的資料，不倚賴應用程式端的檢查。
  • 登入憑證存放在裝置作業系統的安全儲存區（iOS 鑰匙圈／Android Keystore），不與一般 App 資料放在一起。
  • 後端具有最高權限的金鑰只存在於伺服器環境變數中，從不隨 App 發佈。
  • 購買憑證一律在伺服器端向 Apple 或 Google 驗證簽章後才生效，不接受用戶端自行宣稱。

即便如此，沒有任何系統能保證絕對安全。若發生危及你權益的個人資料事故， 我們將依法通知你與主管機關（於歐盟境內為知悉後 72 小時內）。

  • All network traffic uses TLS.
  • The database enforces row-level security, so each user can read only their own rows at the database layer rather than relying on application checks.
  • Sign-in tokens are held in the operating system’s secure store (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), separate from ordinary app data.
  • Highly privileged backend keys exist only in server environment variables and never ship with the App.
  • Purchase receipts are verified server-side against Apple or Google signatures before any entitlement is granted; client claims alone are never trusted.

No system can be guaranteed absolutely secure. If a personal data breach occurs that affects your rights, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority as required by law (within 72 hours of becoming aware, in the EU).
